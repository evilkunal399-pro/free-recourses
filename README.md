# ClassPlus Pro - 100% Free Teaching, Video Lectures & Batch Community Chat

A complete Classplus-style teaching portal featuring in-browser recorded video lectures, downloadable PDF notes & DPPs, and real-time multi-student group discussions.

## Key Features
1. **Video Lecture Player:** Chapter playlist, scrub bar, speed adjustment (1x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 2x), and chapter tracking.
2. **Multi-Student Community Chat:** Open batch study group with avatars, student names, and realtime replies.
3. **Teacher Announcements:** Broadcast channel for official schedules and exam notices.
4. **1-on-1 Ask Teacher Doubts:** Confidential private doubt box between student and teacher.
5. **Downloadable Study Materials:** Real downloadable PDF revision notes, formula sheets, and DPPs.
6. **Teacher vs. Student Switcher:** Toggle roles in one click to test admin lecture management.

## 100% Free Deployment (Zero Server Cost)
- **Frontend Hosting:** Upload `index.html` to [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/) or [Vercel](https://vercel.com/) (100% free forever with custom domains & SSL).
- **Video Hosting:** Embed Unlisted YouTube videos or Google Drive video links for free unlimited streaming.
- **Realtime Multi-Student Database:** Connect [Firebase Realtime Database](https://firebase.google.com/) or [Supabase](https://supabase.com/) free tier (supports up to 50,000 monthly active users at ₹0).
